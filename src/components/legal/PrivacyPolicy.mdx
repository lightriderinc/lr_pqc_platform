## 7. Data Retention

**Uploaded files and scan results.** The file or archive you upload is
extracted and scanned in temporary storage on our servers and is automatically
and permanently deleted immediately after the scan completes or fails —
typically within minutes, and always as part of the same request. We do not
keep a copy of your uploaded content or the resulting findings in any
database. Your findings are returned to your browser for you to view and
optionally download; once you close or refresh the page, we retain no copy of
them.

<br />

**Other data.** Standard server/access logs (e.g. IP address, timestamp,
request metadata) are retained for a shorter period, typically no more than 12
months, unless a longer period is needed for security investigations or legal
compliance. Records of communications you send us (e.g. support requests) are
retained for as long as necessary to address your inquiry and comply with
legal obligations. When no longer needed, data is securely deleted or
anonymized.
